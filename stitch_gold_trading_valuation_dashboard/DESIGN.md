## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the sovereign precision, quiet wealth, and scientific transparency demanded by tier-one bullion dealers, vault custodians, and institutional gold trading desks. The emotional core is institutional gravitas meets Swiss assay lab accuracy: absolute clarity, pristine luminosity, and zero visual clutter. It rejects heavy, ominous dark-mode financial tropes in favor of an airy, warm architectural white space reminiscent of a sunlit vault room in Zurich or Mayfair.

Key aesthetic drivers:
- **Luminous High-Fidelity Minimalism:** Warm pearl and off-white backdrops with hairline metallic transitions that prioritize content legibility over ornamentation.
- **Micro-Precision Metrology:** Visual weight balances the physical density of gold with laboratory-grade calculations (hydrostatic displacement, specific gravity, troy ounces, fineness indices).
- **Executive Restraint:** Champagne and brushed gold are applied strictly as micro-accents, data highlights, and active state indicators—never as broad decorative wash or cheap gradient overlays.
- **Institutional Confidence:** Crisp contrast ratios, rigorous tabular alignment, and authoritative structural framing evoke the dependability of central bank settlement systems.

## Colors

The palette relies on luminous warm pearls, hairline warm grey framing, and resolute obsidian typography to sustain a museum-grade visual field.

### Primary Metallic Tones
- **Brushed Champagne Primary (`#C5A059`):** Core active states, verified hallmark badges, focus rings, and primary interactive highlights.
- **Fine Gold Secondary (`#D4AF37`):** Fine spot-price tickers, live market fix delta indicators, and premium metric rules.
- **Deep Burnished Gold (`#997328`):** Micro-labels, active hover borders, and high-emphasis financial copy requiring AAA accessibility against pale backgrounds.
- **Gold Mist Tint (`#FBF6E9`):** Subtle active selection fills, assay card highlights, and pending-order badge containers.

### Dark Obsidian & Structural Inks
- **Primary Ink (`#111827`):** Primary numeric readouts, ledger records, primary headlines, and dominant navigation nodes.
- **Muted Slate Ink (`#1F2937`):** Secondary headlines, column headers, and active trade order descriptors.
- **Graphite Neutral (`#4B5563`):** Metadata, weight units (ozt, g, kg), timestamp stamps, and secondary table labels.
- **Subtle Silver Hairline (`#9CA3AF`):** Disabled values, dormant ticks, and secondary axis guides.

### Pearl Canvas & Framing Tiers
- **Base Canvas (`#FAF9F6`):** Warm alabaster backdrop providing foundational warmth without yellowing.
- **Elevated Slab (`#FFFFFF`):** Instrument cards, ledger row elevated states, trading ticket surfaces, and modal sheets.
- **Recessed Tray (`#F4F3EF`):** Hydrostatic calculator readouts, data gutters, and fixed header shells.
- **Subtle Architectural Border (`#E8E6DF`):** Standard 1px structural framing, table partition rules, and input boundaries.

### Performance Accents
- **Assay Emerald (`#059669`):** Spot price gains, positive settlement margins, buy-side liquidity spreads, and genuine assay authenticity marks.
- **Emerald Mist (`#ECFDF5`):** Positive delta pill fills and verified ledger settlement indicators.
- **Refined Carmine (`#DC2626`):** Negative settlement delta, sell spread dips, and purity variance alerts.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples modern structural geometry with high-frequency quantitative precision.

- **Headlines & Executive Identity:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides clean, proportional authority with contemporary geometric balance. Kerning is intentionally tightened across display tiers to avoid airy consumer styling and mimic precision engraving.
- **Body & Operational Copy:** Inter provides neutral, highly legible prose across long settlement stipulations, custody disclosures, and audit receipts.
- **Financial Tickers & Metrology Data:** JetBrains Mono is mandatory for all price fixing quotations, hydrostatic scale readouts, density computations, serial numbers, and vault bar tallies. Always activate OpenType tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums`) across all numerical treatments to maintain vertical alignment in ledgers.
- **Micro-Labels & Field Headers:** All field captions and table column heads employ uppercase styling with an expanded letter spacing of `+0.08em` for readability at small scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture operates on an eight-point fractional grid system with high horizontal breathing room to reflect institutional scale.

- **Desktop (1440px and up):** 12-column symmetrical fluid grid. Maximum container width caps at 1440px to prevent visual separation between execution inputs and market ledger feeds. Canvas margins standardise at `margin-desktop` (48px) with internal column gutters at `gutter-lg` (32px).
- **Tablet / Workstation (768px – 1439px):** 8-column layout. Margin resets to 32px with 24px gutters. Analytical charts and settlement tables adapt horizontally with fixed headers and frozen key columns.
- **Mobile Handheld (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout. Canvas margins contract to `margin-mobile` (16px) and gutters to `gutter-sm` (16px). Dual-metric calculators stack vertically into dedicated segmented cards.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Density remains uniform. Structural cards apply `space-lg` (24px) internal padding, while analytical readouts, formula results, and financial line items operate on tighter `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px) vertical rhythms to maintain unified visual groups.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows and murky underlays. Depth is communicated strictly through surface layering and luminous ambient refraction.

- **The Plane Hierarchy:** 
  - *Base Substrate (`#FAF9F6`):* Underlying screen foundation.
  - *Slab Tier (`#FFFFFF`):* Primary structural cards, forms, and analytical modules resting flush with the substrate, outlined with a 1px border of `#E8E6DF`.
  - *Elevated Inspector Panel:* 1px border in `#E8E6DF`, backed by a warm ambient diffusion shadow: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.06)`.
- **Active Trade Focus & Overlays:** Modals, trade execution drawdowns, and settlement popovers use a micro-tinted glass backdrop: `#FAF9F6` at 85% opacity paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(140%)` and framed with an active champagne border (`#C5A059`).
- **Precision Recess (Counters & Insets):** Weight measurement fields, assay readouts, and specific gravity calculation cards utilize a recessed visual style via `#F4F3EF` fill with an inset stroke: `inset 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a tailored, disciplined geometric shape profile (`roundedness: 1`). Radii are deliberately understated to evoke the clean, machined edges of a cast 400 oz London Good Delivery bullion bar.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Standard interactive elements, including inline input fields, dropdown triggers, numeric pill badges, assay data tags, and ticker status flags.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Primary ledger tables, custody order cards, trade entry panels, and hydrostatic analysis modules.
- **Modal Radius (`rounded-xl`, 0.75rem / 12px):** Global trade confirmation modals, certificate inspection dialogs, and biometric verification sheets.
- **Zero Radius Elements:** Table inner cell dividers, ticker strip boundaries, and metric separation rules remain strictly sharp (`0px`) to maintain architectural rigor.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Execution Button (Buy/Allocate Gold):** Obsidian solid (`#111827`) with crisp pearl text (`#FAF9F6`), 40px height, 4px corner radius. On hover, background shifts to `#1F2937` with an active 1px perimeter glow in `#C5A059`.
- **Secondary Liquidation Button (Sell/Redeem):** Crisp pearl surface (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px hairline border (`#E8E6DF`), text in `#111827`. Hover applies border `#C5A059` and text `#997328`.
- **Assay Verification CTA:** Champagne accent base (`#C5A059`) with bold slate ink (`#111827`), used exclusively for confirming purity assays, locking fixing orders, and executing physical delivery allocations.

### Precision Dual-Weight Inputs & Hydrostatic Metric Cards
- **Input Architecture:** Dual-labeled field structures designed for precious metals authentication.
- **Container Format:** 48px height, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px perimeter of `#E8E6DF`. Focus state triggers a 1px border of `#C5A059` and a soft ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.15)`.
- **Dual Metric Grouping:** Side-by-side inputs for *Dry Weight in Air ($W_{\text{air}}$)* and *Hydrostatic Weight in Water ($W_{\text{water}}$)* featuring trailing mono unit badges (`g` or `ozt`) in JetBrains Mono (`#4B5563`).
- **Calculated Metric Bar (Specific Gravity & Purity):** Recessed panel in `#F4F3EF` immediately underneath inputs. Displays calculated density ($\rho = \frac{W_{\text{air}}}{W_{\text{air}} - W_{\text{water}}}$) alongside computed karat fineness (e.g., `19.32 g/cm³ → 24K Pure (999.9 Fine)`). When density matches pure gold parameters ($\pm 0.05$), the value renders in `#059669` accompanied by a verified check icon; variances outside acceptable tolerance shift values to `#DC2626`.

### Price Schedule & London Fix Tickers
- **Market Ticker Strip:** Horizontal ticker strip set against `#FFFFFF` with a 1px bottom border in `#E8E6DF`. Ticker items show instrument code (`XAU/USD`, `LBMA AM`, `LBMA PM`), primary price in JetBrains Mono bold (`#111827`), followed by an emerald pill (`#ECFDF5`) containing the delta value (`+$14.20 / +0.58%`).
- **Fixing Status:** Active live trading is marked by a 6px pulsating ring badge in `#059669`. Off-market hours display a static dot in `#9CA3AF`.

### Ledger Tables & Settlement Records
- **Table Construction:** Flush border layout with `#FFFFFF` rows. Header cells utilize `#FAF9F6` background, 11px uppercase `label-caps` typography in `#4B5563`, and 36px height.
- **Data Rows:** 48px height, padded with 16px horizontal spacing. Dividing lines are 1px `#E8E6DF`. Numeric fields (Troy Ounces, Assay Purity, Premium over Spot, Total USD Settlement) align right in JetBrains Mono.
- **Interactive Row Selection:** Hovering shifts row background to `#FBF6E9` at 50% opacity, revealing an inline "Allocate to Vault" micro-action.

### Checkboxes, Radio Buttons & Segmented Switches
- **Form Selectors:** Square-rounded 16px controls with 1px `#E8E6DF` border. Checked states feature solid `#111827` fill with a sharp white tick mark, or an inner `#C5A059` pip for radio buttons.
- **Unit Segmented Control (ozt / g / kg):** Recessed pill container (`#F4F3EF`) housing flat tab segments. Active selection snaps forward in `#FFFFFF` with 2px corner radius, hairline shadow, and `#111827` semibold text.

### Assay Identity Chips & Hallmarks
- **Purity Badges:** Micro-pills (20px height, 4px radius) indicating fineness (`999.9`, `999.5`, `916.7`). Default background is `#FBF6E9` with deep gold typography (`#997328`) and a 1px border of `#C5A059`.
